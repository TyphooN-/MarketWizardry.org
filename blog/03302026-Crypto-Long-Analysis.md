## The Supply Mirror

The SOL short worked because of supply dynamics — 4% annual inflation with no cap means $1.65B of new SOL floods the market every year. When demand collapses, constant supply accelerates the decline. The cascade exploits this acceleration.

The long thesis is the mirror image. You want supply dynamics that **amplify** recovery — where every dollar of new demand moves price maximally because supply is not fighting you.

This immediately sorts the 7 cryptos into tiers:

**S-Tier (Supply works FOR you):**
- **BTC:** Sub-1% inflation, hard cap at 21M, halving schedule, production cost floor at ~$75K. The tightest supply in crypto. Every dollar of institutional money that flows through ETFs hits a fixed supply wall.

**A-Tier (Minimal supply headwind):**
- **ETH:** ~0.23% net emission post-Merge. Staking locks 29% of supply. Near-zero dilution.
- **BNB:** Actively deflationary — quarterly burns reducing supply toward 100M target. Supply shrinks while you are long.

**B-Tier (Manageable dilution):**
- **ADA:** 2.45% declining. Has a hard cap at 45B and reserves are depleting. Dilution expires eventually.

**C-Tier (Supply fights recovery):**
- **SOL:** 4% inflation, no cap. The same emission that made it the best short candidate makes it a mediocre long.
- **XRP:** Ripple's 35B escrow overhang. Monthly unlocks. Centralized supply control.

**D-Tier (Structural dilution, no catalyst):**
- **DOGE:** 3.4% forever. No cap. No burn. No plan. Five billion new DOGE per year until the heat death of the universe.

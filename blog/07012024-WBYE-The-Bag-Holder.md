## Lessons Learned — "Everything I Needed to Know About Failure"

### 1. Diversification Doesn't Fix a Broken Strategy

46 symbols. -$246K. If your strategy loses money, more symbols means more ways to lose money. Fix the strategy first. Then diversify.

### 2. The LAZR Addiction Must Be Addressed

13,080 trades. Negative P/L. Across FOUR DARWINs. At some point, "the setup looks good" stops being a reason and starts being a delusion. If a symbol is consistently losing money across multiple independent systems, the symbol is the problem. Ban it.

### 3. One Good Month Creates Dangerous Delusion

August 2024: +$213K. This one month kept WBYE alive long after it should have been shut down. "But we had that great August!" became the excuse for every subsequent loss. One good month is not a strategy. One good month is variance. Don't confuse the two.

### 4. Being Close to Profitable Is Sometimes Worse Than Being Far Away

WBYE is 3.4 percentage points from breakeven. This proximity creates a perpetual "almost there" mentality that prevents decisive action. If WBYE were losing like XUQF (-80% DD, 22% win rate), the decision to shut it down would be obvious. WBYE's proximity to profitability makes it a zombie that's just sentient enough to argue it should keep living.

### 5. The Largest Loss Defines Your Risk More Than the Average Loss

Average loss: $367. Largest loss: $67,370. That's a 183x multiplier between average and worst case. Your risk management needs to handle the $67K day, not the $367 day. WBYE's position sizing was calibrated for the average case and got obliterated by the worst case.

### 6. Diversification Without Edge Is Just Organized Failure

46 symbols with no edge on half of them is worse than 18 symbols with genuine edge on all of them. Every symbol without edge is a drag on the symbols WITH edge. WBYE's 46-symbol portfolio diluted whatever genuine edge existed with 30+ symbols of pure noise.

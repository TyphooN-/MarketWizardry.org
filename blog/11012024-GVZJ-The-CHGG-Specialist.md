## Lessons Learned — "Know Thyself (And Thy Stock)"

### 1. Specialization Works If You Actually Have Edge

73% on one stock produced $143K. This ONLY works because the EA has genuine, measurable, repeatable edge on CHGG. If GVZJ had 73% on a stock where it had NO edge, it would look like XUQF. Know where your edge is. Deploy capital there. Nowhere else.

### 2. Win Rate Isn't the Flex You Think It Is

49.06% win rate sounds impressive until you realize HAKR made 8x more money at 29.41%. High win rate with small edge = small profit. Low win rate with large edge = large profit. Choose wisely.

### 3. One Month Can Make Your Year (or Your Career)

January 2026: +$209K. More than the total lifetime profit. If GVZJ had been turned off in December 2025, it would be a failure. The lesson: you can't predict WHEN your edge will pay off. You can only ensure you're positioned when it does.

### 4. Know Your Instrument Better Than Anyone

5,370 trades on one stock means GVZJ's algorithm has seen every CHGG pattern, every earnings reaction, every gap fill, every mean reversion. 5,370 data points of live market experience on one instrument is worth more than 100 data points on 50 instruments. Depth beats breadth.

### 5. The Risk Is Real

$40,500 largest loss on (probably) one stock. 33.41% max drawdown driven by (probably) one stock's bad month. Specialization concentrates returns AND risk. You don't get to keep the upside of concentration without accepting the downside. If you can't stomach a $40K loss on one position, don't put 73% on one stock.

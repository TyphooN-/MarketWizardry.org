## Lessons Learned — "What WBYE Taught At $6.01/Trade In Profit"

### 1. 46 Symbols Is Not Diversification. It's Dilution.
You cannot have edge on 46 stocks simultaneously. You have edge on maybe 5-10. The other 36 are noise that dilutes the winners and adds extra losing positions. WBYE found edge on LUMN (+$421K) and CC (+$173K). Every other symbol was a tax on that edge.

### 2. The Long Bias Amplifies Every Mistake
85.8% long. When the market goes down, WBYE goes down harder. When NVDA shorts, WBYE gets cremated. The directional bias is fine in bull markets. In choppy markets — which is what stocks are most of the time — being 85.8% long means you're fighting the variance with one arm tied behind your back.

### 3. -$67,390 On One NVDA Short Is Not A Risk Management Decision. It's A Crime.
The largest single loss across all stock DARWINs (XUQF's individual losses are smaller per trade). $67,390 from one NVDA short. How does this happen? Position sizing that didn't account for NVIDIA's volatility and momentum. The stop was too wide, or there wasn't one. This is the exact trade that makes risk management people have aneurysms.

### 4. Your Best Symbol Can't Save You If Everything Else Is On Fire
LUMN: +$421K. CC: +$173K. CHGG: +$90K. CRWD: +$8K. Total from profitable symbols: ~$700K+.

Net result: +$51K. It took 20 months, but the winners finally won.

The losing symbols still extracted massive capital from this account. WBYE's winners were carrying an entire failing portfolio on their back — and eventually carried it into the green. The best trade ideas were gasping for oxygen under the weight of 2,616 LAZR trades losing $224K. The lesson: fix the losers faster. The winners will eventually save you, but the ride will be ugly.

### 5. Month One Is Not The Strategy
-$329K in July 2024 is not evidence the strategy doesn't work. It's evidence that WBYE's algorithm had a rough market regime in month one. August 2024 (+$181K) is evidence the strategy can work. Don't judge the strategy by month one. Judge it by the 20-month equity curve — which, admittedly, is also bad, but at least it shows why it's bad.
